About Kenable

Technology adapted to meaningful needs.

Advances in sensing, wireless connectivity, edge computing, artificial intelligence and cloud platforms are creating new possibilities. Kenable works at the point where these technologies are mature enough to be adapted for practical use.

Our emphasis is on useful, dependable and context-appropriate solutions that can improve safety, efficiency, quality of life, learning or resource utilisation.
